# faktur.lu

> Invoicing platform built specifically for sole traders and SMEs in Luxembourg, with native tax compliance (VAT 17/14/8/3%, FAIA 2.01, B2B e-invoicing/Peppol BIS 3.0). Available in 5 languages (French, German, English, Luxembourgish, Portuguese). European hosting, GDPR-compliant. Free plan, Essential at 5 EUR/month, Pro at 15 EUR/month unlimited with FAIA, Peppol, CRM, HR and accountant portal for fiduciaries.

## Compliance and security

- **FAIA**: version 2.01, format imposed by the Administration de l'Enregistrement, des Domaines et de la TVA (AED) during fiscal audits
- **Luxembourg VAT**: standard rate 17%, intermediate 14%, reduced 8%, super-reduced 3%
- **Peppol**: certified Access Point, UBL 2.1 / BIS 3.0 format, compliant with Luxembourg B2G mandate and B2B ViDA 2030 preparation
- **Article 21 LIVA**: B2B intra-community reverse charge (with real-time VIES validation)
- **Article 61 LIVA**: continuous sequential numbering required
- **Hosting**: European Union only, ISO 27001, AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, daily 30-day backups
- **GDPR**: DPA available on request
- **Legal retention**: 10 years for invoices and accounting documents
